Sector valuation ranges

  • Software/Tech: about 28–35x — technology often trades at a premium because investors expect strong earnings growth.
  • Semiconductor Equipment: among the highest of any industry group — semiconductor equipment and materials have averaged around 63.04x.
  • Utilities: about 18x — lower growth, but valued for stability and dividend income.
  • Airlines/Cyclicals: around 7x — lower valuations reflect cyclical risk and capital intensity.
  • Mortgage Finance / Reinsurance: among the lowest — mortgage finance has averaged around 9x, while insurance-reinsurance is around 10.22x.
  • S&P 500 overall: historically around 15–18x, but in early 2026 it traded closer to 22–24x due to the heavy weighting toward growth and technology.

Source: Basis Report
