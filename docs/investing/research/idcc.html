What changed in this version

This page was rebuilt using only InterDigital's SEC filings (10-K and 10-Q data as filed). The earlier version used a third-party WACC estimate, and it did not deduct share-based pay. (3) Cash and investments, debt and shares are updated to Sep 30, 2026.

Method

Because the filings do not support a reliable multi-year operating forecast for a company this cyclical or lumpy, the value is the present value of a flat perpetual stream of FCF (after share-based pay and capitalized patent costs) at four levels taken directly from the filings, plus cash and investments less debt, divided by shares. No growth is assumed (g = 0). Where the resulting value is above the $105.30 warrant strike, shares are increased by the warrants' dilution (treasury method, exact for each value).

The four levels are: Bear = the weakest of the last three fiscal years; Conservative = the median year; Base = the 3-year average; Run-rate = the latest twelve months. Present value = FCF ÷ discount rate.

Caveats

  • The convertible notes carry a note hedge, which offsets the value of the shares owed above principal when holders convert. I treat that offset as complete and count only the principal as a cash liability; if the hedge did not fully offset, the equity value would be much lower. The 10-Q excerpt used does not value the hedge.
  • Payments for patent acquisitions ($8.8M in 2025) are not deducted; capitalized patent costs are.
  • The Samsung arbitration set royalties of $1.05 billion for an eight-year license in July 2025, which Samsung is challenging; the Lenovo result is expected in the first half of 2027. These outcomes are not in the history.
  • Operating cash flow depends on when licensees pay, so a single year can be far above or below revenue; nine-month 2026 operating cash flow was $249.0M against $481.1M a year earlier.
  • Cash and debt are taken at the dates shown; any growth beyond flat FCF is excluded, so a company that keeps compounding will be worth more than shown.
  • The rating holds for any price between $181 and $362.
