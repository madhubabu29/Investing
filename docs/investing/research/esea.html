Model-based rating: BUY (value signal) — higher risk

Rule used: compare the price with the three scenario values at a 10% discount rate. ESEA's price of $66.58 is below the bear-case value of $76 (conservative $130, base $170). The price only equals the bear value at about an 11.5% discount rate. The market is pricing in re-charter rates about 35% below today's fixtures, or a discount rate above 12%. About 65% of 2027 ship-days and $600M of revenue are already under contract, and the 12 newbuilds add ships from 2027.

What would change this: the rating would drop to Hold if the price rose toward the conservative value ($110–$155), or if container charter rates fall by more than 35% on re-chartering from 2028 (at 50% of today's rates the value is about $35 at 10%). Main risks: 44% of 2028 ship-days are contracted; the order book needs $479.7M of cash and new debt; and the Pittas family owns 58.9%, so minority holders have limited influence.

Method

Every ship is modeled month by month. Existing ships earn their listed charter rate until the contract ends (April 15 fleet table), then the "re-charter" rate, which is the ship's latest contract rate times the scenario factor. Newbuilds start in their delivery quarter; the four 4,484 TEU ships have charters at $35,500 per day, and the other eight re-charter at the latest rates for their size ($30,000 for 2,800 TEU, $23,500 for 1,800 TEU). Cash margin per ship-day = rate × 99.5% utilization × (1 − 2.8% commission) − $7,053 operating and management cost − $1,072 drydock (average of FY2024 and FY2025), less $6.9M a year of G&A. Each ship earns until age 25 (never less than its contract), with no scrap value, no growth and no ships beyond the order book. Equity value = present value of these flows − present value of the $479.7M installments + $203.2M cash and securities − $208.1M debt − $5.0M minority interest, divided by 7.06M shares.

Check: contracted revenue in this model is $571M against the 6-K's $600M. The gap is charters fixed after April 15, so the model is slightly conservative.

Caveats
