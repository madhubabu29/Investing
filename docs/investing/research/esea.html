Method

Every ship is modeled month by month. Existing ships earn their listed charter rate until the contract ends (Nov 17 fleet table), then the "re-charter" rate, which is the ship's latest contract rate times the scenario factor. Newbuilds start in their delivery quarter; the four 4,484 TEU ships have charters at $35,500 per day, and the other eight re-charter at the latest fixtures for their size ($30,000 for 2,800 TEU and $25,500 for 1,800 TEU). Cash margin per ship-day = rate × 99.5% utilization × (1 − 2.8% commission) − $7,045 operating and management cost − $1,072 drydock, less $6.6M a year of G&A ($898 per ship-day on 20 ships). Each ship earns until age 25 (never less than its contract), with no scrap value, no growth and no ships beyond the order book. Equity value = present value of these flows − present value of the newbuild installments + $244.0M cash and securities (including the ship sale) − $203.6M debt − $5.0M minority interest, divided by 7.06M shares.

  • The $26M sale proceeds were received after Sep 30 and are added to cash; sale costs are not deducted. The $0.85 dividend payable Dec 16 is not deducted.
  • The 49% minority partner in the ship Thrylos is handled by deducting its book value ($5.0M); the effect is under $1 per share.
  • Newbuild costs not yet paid are assumed to be paid from cash, with no new debt.
